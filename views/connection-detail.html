Timeouts and retries

Only GET, HEAD, OPTIONS, PUT and DELETE are retried, after network errors or the listed statuses, waiting 100 ms, 200 ms, 400 ms… between attempts.

Circuit breaker

Stops calling a failing backend for a while, so clients get a fast 503 instead of piling up on timeouts.

Upstream credentials

Added to every upstream request, unless a policy sets that header itself.

TLS

For https:// upstreams. Use your own CA for private backends and a client certificate when the backend requires mTLS.
